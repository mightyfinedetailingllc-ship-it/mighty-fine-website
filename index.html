GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Everything you need before reaching out.

Are the website prices guaranteed?

No. All listed prices are starting prices. Final pricing depends on vehicle size, condition and the cleaning required. Any excess cleaning is quoted separately before the additional work is performed.

Where do you provide mobile detailing?

We serve Wayne, Medina, Stark and Summit counties. If you're unsure whether your location is in range, send us your city and we'll confirm.

Do I have to book online?

No. Call, text or email Mighty Fine directly. We'll work with you to choose the right service and schedule.

Do you detail motorcycles, boats or RVs?

Yes. Motorcycles, boats and RVs are priced by estimate. Contact us with the details and we'll provide a quote.
